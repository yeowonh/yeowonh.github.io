## Problem

Most assistants forget you between sessions. Every conversation restarts from nothing, which is fine
for a search box and useless for anything that is supposed to feel like a friend.

The goal for My Bestie was a chatbot that remembers one person over time. The product focused on
continuity and personalization rather than treating every session as a fresh search query.

## Approach

- **Scoped the experience around continuity.** The team treated long-term personalization as the
  core product problem, not an optional layer on top of a generic assistant.
- **Delivered as a seven-person NLP team.** We kept the one-month project focused enough to reach a
  public exhibition and deployment.

## Result

- Completed within the one-month final project window and taken through **public exhibition and
  deployment** instead of stopping at a demo notebook.
