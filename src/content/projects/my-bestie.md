## Problem

Most assistants treat every session as independent. That works for search, but not for a product
designed around continuity and personalization.

The goal for My Bestie was a chatbot that remembers one person over time. The product focused on
continuity and personalization rather than treating every session as a fresh search query.

## Approach

- **Scoped the experience around continuity.** The team treated long-term personalization as the
  core product problem, not an optional layer on top of a generic assistant.
- **Delivered by a seven-person NLP team.** We kept the one-month project focused enough to reach a
  public exhibition and deployment.

## Result

- **Delivery velocity | Shipped a public deployment in four weeks** within the Boostcamp final
  project window.
- **Team execution | Delivered with a seven-person NLP team,** keeping the scope focused on memory
  and continuity instead of a broad generic assistant.
- **Product validation | Advanced beyond a demo notebook to public exhibition,** putting the full
  personalized conversation flow in front of real visitors.
